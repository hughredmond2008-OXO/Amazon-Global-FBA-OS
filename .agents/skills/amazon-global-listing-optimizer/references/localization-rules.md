# Localization Rules

## General
Localize meaning, not words. Preserve factual specifications while adapting vocabulary, spelling, syntax, units, and shopping language.

## United States
Use US English and terminology natural to US Amazon shoppers.

## Canada
Use Canadian terminology and spelling where natural, with appropriate metric usage and bilingual considerations where relevant.

## United Kingdom
Use UK English spelling and terminology and metric units as appropriate.

## Australia
Use Australian English, Australian spelling, locally natural terminology, and metric measurements where appropriate.

## Germany
Use natural German e-commerce terminology rather than literal English translation. Preserve technical specifications accurately.

## Japan
Use natural Japanese e-commerce language and keep model numbers and technical specifications precise.

## Other marketplaces
Identify local language, spelling, unit conventions, search terminology, and category vocabulary before drafting. If reliable information is unavailable, state the uncertainty.

## Claims
Localization must never increase the factual strength of a claim. A benefit must not become a guarantee, certification, safety claim, medical claim, or performance promise.
